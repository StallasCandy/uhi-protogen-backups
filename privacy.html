Privacy policy · 30 September 2026

Your backups, your account.

This policy describes UHI Protogen Backups, a personal backup utility operated by StallasCandy.

Data accessed and purpose

The utility uploads selected worker backup archives and accesses their contents and file metadata as needed to create, check, manage and restore backups. Archives may contain application data and saved memories selected by the operator. Google Drive access is requested using the drive.file scope, covering files created by or explicitly shared with the app.

Storage and access

Backup data is stored on the worker and in the operator's Google Drive account. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in the worker's private configuration to allow authorised backup operations. Google and the worker's hosting provider process data as part of providing their services. Backup contents are not published through this website.

Use and sharing

Data accessed through Google Drive is used for backup management and recovery. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train general-purpose AI models. This utility does not share backup contents with other users.

Retention and deletion

Archives remain until deleted by the operator or removed by a configured retention rule. No fixed automatic retention period is currently promised. The operator can delete backups in Google Drive and remove local copies from the worker. Files placed in Drive's trash remain subject to Google's deletion process.

Revoking access

The operator can revoke the app's access through their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access stops future authorised Drive operations but does not itself delete existing archives.

This website

This static information website has no sign-in form, tracking scripts or analytics added by the operator. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process technical request information under its own privacy policy.
